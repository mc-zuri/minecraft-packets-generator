# Third-party sources

The generator is derived from PrismarineJS/minecraft-data-generator and its mc-zuri fork.
Its MIT license and original attribution are retained in `LICENSE`.

Packet streams are compressed with [zstd-jni](https://github.com/luben/zstd-jni)
(BSD 2-Clause), a Maven dependency of `common`; it bundles the zstd library (BSD).
